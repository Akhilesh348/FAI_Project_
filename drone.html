Q-Learning for Drone Navigation

Works well for small state spaces. Learns to avoid obstacles but may struggle with battery optimization in large grids.

Challenge: Limited to discrete state representations

SARSA for Drone Navigation

On-policy approach leads to more cautious flight paths, reducing collision risk.

Benefit: Safer navigation near obstacles

Monte Carlo for Drone Navigation

Episode-based learning evaluates complete flight paths. Better battery management through full trajectory optimization.

Advantage: Optimizes entire delivery path

DQN for Complex Navigation

Excels at handling complex obstacle patterns and continuous state spaces. Best for realistic drone navigation.

Strength: Learns efficient paths in complex environments
